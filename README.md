# Hi, I'm Meenhal 👋

Software engineering student, with a BSc in Computer Science.
I make mobile apps and websites, and I like taking them all the way: design, code, store release, then updates.

📍 France · 🌐 French, English

## 🚀 Projects

**📱 [Wasilah](https://wasilah.app)** · React Native, Expo, TypeScript
<br />
An app to read the Quran and supplications in Arabic, with translations. It sends a verse every morning and reminders at prayer times.
I made it on my own and it's live on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6758405533) and [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.meenhal.wasilah).

**⌚ [Qaza](https://meenhal.dev/app/qaza/)** · Swift
<br />
Keeps count of the prayers and fasts you missed, so you can make them up at your own pace.
Works on iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch, syncs with iCloud, no account needed.

**🌐 App websites** · React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind
<br />
The sites for [Wasilah](https://wasilah.app) and [Qaza](https://meenhal.dev/app/qaza/), with their privacy and support pages. I host them myself on a VPS with Nginx.
